# Dinosaur Adjectives – Year 2 Teacher Guide

## Learning intention
I can identify and use adjectives to describe nouns.

## Success criteria
- I can spot adjectives in simple sentences.
- I can choose adjectives that fit a noun.
- I can add adjectives to make a sentence more interesting.

## Suggested lesson flow
1. **Starter (5–10 min):** Show or draw a dinosaur and collect describing words as a class.
2. **Model (5 min):** Explain that an adjective tells us more about a noun. Model examples such as *a fierce dinosaur* and *sharp teeth*.
3. **Guided practice (10 min):** Complete the adjective hunt together.
4. **Independent practice (10–15 min):** Pupils complete the sentence gaps and improve plain sentences.
5. **Challenge (10 min):** Pupils draw a dinosaur and write 3–5 descriptive sentences using at least five adjectives.
6. **Plenary (5 min):** Invite pupils to share one noun and adjective pair.

## Useful vocabulary
huge, tiny, green, fierce, scaly, long, sharp, noisy, fast, slow, spiky, strong, enormous, colourful

## Differentiation
### Support
- Give pupils a reduced word bank of 5–6 adjectives.
- Let pupils orally rehearse sentences before writing.
- Use sentence stems such as: *The ___ dinosaur has ___ teeth.*

### Core
- Ask pupils to choose suitable adjectives independently.
- Encourage one or two adjectives before a noun.

### Challenge
- Ask pupils to use two adjectives separated by a comma, e.g. *an enormous, green dinosaur*.
- Ask pupils to explain why an adjective is suitable.

## Assessment opportunities
Listen for whether pupils can distinguish the naming word (noun) from the describing word (adjective). Check that chosen adjectives make sense in context and are placed correctly in a sentence.
