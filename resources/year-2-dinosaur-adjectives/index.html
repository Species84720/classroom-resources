Dinosaur Adjectives

Year 2 English · Learning intention: I can identify and use adjectives to describe nouns.

1. Build a dinosaur word bank

What words could describe a dinosaur?

hugegreenfiercescalylongsharpnoisyfastspikystrong

2. Adjective hunt

Underline the adjective in each sentence.

  1. The huge dinosaur stomped through the forest.
  2. A tiny dinosaur hid behind a rock.
  3. The T. rex had sharp teeth.
  4. The green dinosaur had a long tail.
  5. A noisy dinosaur roared loudly.

3. Complete the descriptions

Choose a suitable adjective from the word bank.

The Triceratops had three horns.

The dinosaur had teeth.

A Stegosaurus walked past the trees.

Its tail swung from side to side.

The dinosaur gave a loud roar.

4. Make it better

Add at least one adjective to each sentence.

  • I saw a dinosaur.
  • The dinosaur had teeth.
  • It walked through the jungle.
  • It had a tail.

Example: I saw an enormous, green dinosaur.

5. My dinosaur challenge

Draw your own dinosaur and write 3–5 sentences describing it. Try to use at least five adjectives.
