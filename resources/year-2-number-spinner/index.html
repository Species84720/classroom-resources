Number Spinner

Year 2 Maths · Learning intention: I can use numbers to practise place value, comparison and simple calculations.

1. Spin a number

10
20
30
40

Press the button to choose one of the spinner numbers.

?

2. Number detective

Use the number you spin.

  1. What number is 10 more?
  2. What number is 10 less?
  3. How many tens are in the number?
  4. Write the number in words.
  5. Is it greater than, less than or equal to 30?

3. Spin twice

Spin two numbers and use them to answer the questions.

  1. Add the two numbers.
  2. Find the difference between the two numbers.
  3. Which number is greater?
  4. Put the two numbers in ascending order.

4. Challenge

Choose one spun number and make as many different number sentences as you can that equal it.

Example for 20: 10 + 10 = 20, 30 − 10 = 20, 5 + 15 = 20.

5. Success check

✓ I can read numbers in tens.
✓ I can find 10 more and 10 less.
✓ I can compare two numbers.
✓ I can add and subtract simple multiples of 10.
