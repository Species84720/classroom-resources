Teacher options & how to play

Learning intention: represent numbers to 100 using groups of ten and ones. Each tray contains exactly ten apples. Any correct combination is accepted; after success, compare it with the tens-and-ones form.

Drag with a mouse, touch screen or pen. You can also tap a supply, or focus it and press Enter/Space. Tap a cart item to remove it. Undo reverses the last add, removal or clear.

Set a fixed target with ?target=32 (whole numbers 1–100). Without a valid target, each round chooses a random number. New potion keeps a fixed URL target; otherwise it chooses a different number.
