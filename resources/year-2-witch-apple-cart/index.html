    Teacher options & how to play

    Represent numbers to 100 using groups of ten and ones. Every tray contains exactly ten of the current ingredient. Complete all three ingredients to make the potion; incorrect answers can be corrected. Sound is on and starts with your first tap or key press. Use Hear the request to repeat it.

    Drag with a mouse, touch screen or pen, or tap a supply. Keyboard: focus a supply and press Enter/Space; focus a cart item and press Enter/Space to remove it. Undo reverses an add, removal or clear. Animations on overrides the device’s reduced-motion setting; use Animations off whenever needed.

    Choose the numbers

    Comma-separated whole numbers from 1 to 100. A list takes priority over the range. Numbers are drawn without repeating until the list or range is used up.

    Choose the three ingredients

    With all three set to Random, each potion uses apples, dragon eggs and purple frogs in a random order.

    URL examples: ?min=20&max=50, ?numbers=43,22,64, or ?numbers=43,22,64&items=apples,eggs,frogs. Ingredient choices follow the listed order; a shorter list repeats to fill three questions. ?target=32 keeps the same number for all three questions. Add &motion=off to disable animation.
